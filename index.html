How I work

01

Understand

Start from the real constraint — compliance, engineering, or the user's actual workflow — rather than the brief as written.

02

Design

Prototype against edge cases early: partial permissions, revoked access, skipped steps — the paths most designs forget.

03

Verify

Test assumptions, not just flows. The liveness tutorial case study exists because I checked whether a step was working, not just whether it shipped.
