Liveness & Identity Verification

VIDA Digital Identity — Verify and Authentication

Status: This is the most evidence-backed of my four case studies — the usability testing finding below is real and specific — but the surrounding flow screens and final-state visuals still need to be pulled together.

Context

Liveness verification is the step where a user proves they're a real, present person during identity verification — usually by following on-screen prompts in front of a camera. I worked on this as part of the Verify and Authentication stream with PM Arin, alongside VIDA's brand rollout and broader usability testing on the verification flow.

Problem

Before changing anything, I wanted to know whether the tutorial step that introduces the liveness flow was actually doing its job. Tutorials are easy to design and easy to skip — the question was whether users were engaging with it at all, or just tapping through to get to the task.

What I did

  • Initiated and ran usability testing specifically targeting the liveness tutorial step, rather than assuming it was working based on design intent alone.
  • Found that approximately 90% of users were skipping the tutorial entirely — a signal that the step was adding friction without adding comprehension.
  • Used this finding to argue for a change in design direction: rather than front-loading instructions, shift toward in-context guidance during the liveness capture itself.

Why this matters

This is a small-sounding finding with a real implication: if nearly everyone skips a step meant to prevent errors downstream, the error-prevention has to move somewhere else in the flow — it can't rely on a tutorial no one reads. It's also a case where testing a specific assumption produced a concrete, actionable number rather than general impressions.

Outcome

Evidence gap: The ~90% skip-rate finding itself is solid, but I haven't yet attached the actual tutorial-vs-redesigned screens, or confirmed what shipped as a result and when. That detail will be added once verified — not before.
