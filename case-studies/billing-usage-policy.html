Billing, Usage & Policy Dashboard

VIDA Digital Identity — Self-service Portal

Status: This case study is still being written. The sections below reflect my role and process accurately, but screens, flow diagrams, and before/after comparisons are not yet attached, and I have not yet verified specific usage or adoption numbers. I'd rather leave those blank than guess.

Context

At VIDA, the Self-service Portal lets client organizations manage their own billing, usage monitoring, and policy configuration without needing to go through an account manager. I owned design for this area end to end — discovery, flow mapping, prototyping, usability testing, and engineering handoff — working with PMs Michal, Rezka, Rezha, and Nishant.

Problem

Billing, usage data, and policy settings existed as separate concerns handled by different teams internally, but client-facing users needed to understand all three together: how much they were using, what it was costing them, and what policies governed that usage. The dashboard needed to make those connections legible without requiring a support ticket.

My role

  • Led discovery and flow mapping across billing, usage, and policy configuration.
  • Designed the transaction list and usage summary views, including handling for post-correction and manual review states.
  • Prepared detailed specs and polished visuals for engineering handoff, standardizing components across screens that had previously been designed inconsistently.
  • Explored early concepts using dummy personas to test assumptions before committing to a direction.
